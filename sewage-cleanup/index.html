Of all the problems an owner can have, sewage coming up through the drains ranks among the most dangerous. Anyone who comes in contact with contaminated material is exposed to parasites, bacteria and viruses, among them giardia, E. coli, salmonella and hepatitis, all carried in raw sewage and all dangerous. Across Lynn, Lynn Restoration answers sewage emergencies 24/7, sending trained technicians who arrive with EPA-registered disinfectants and the right protective equipment so your property is decontaminated safely.

Sewage damage is classified as Category 3 (black water), the most contaminated and dangerous category of water damage. Because of it, cleanup follows the toughest rules in the trade, which means full protective gear, barriers to contain the area, tearing out any porous material that met sewage and disinfecting all that is left. Our technicians follow IICRC S500 standards for Category 3 water damage so your property is restored to safe occupancy standards.

We clear the standing sewage first, then pull contaminated water out of structural materials, remove carpet, padding, insulation, drywall and any other porous item that was in contact with sewage for proper disposal, disinfect each hard surface, treat with antimicrobial products and finish with structural drying, which prevents secondary mold. Lingering sewage odor is eliminated with thermal fogging and industrial hydroxyl generators, which we run in the affected area.

Your Sewage Cleanup Questions Answered

Most backups start with a blockage in the home's sewer line. Grease buildup, wipes and other items that should not be flushed, and tree roots growing into the pipe are the usual causes. Other possibilities are a failed old pipe and a municipal sewer overloaded by heavy rain, which sends sewage up through floor drains. To know for certain, have the line camera-inspected once cleanup is finished.
Removing the sewage and decontaminating the property is usually done in 1 to 3 days. Complete restoration, including material replacement, may take 1 to 3 weeks depending on the extent of damage. Our first priority is making the property safe to occupy again as fast as we can.
Temporary relocation is what we usually recommend, particularly for a backup that contaminated a large part of the home. There are health risks from pathogens that become airborne and from the products we use to decontaminate. After seeing your situation firsthand, our team tells you what makes sense.
Nobody, pets included, should walk through the area the sewage reached. Do not flush, run faucets or start the washer or dishwasher, because added water feeds the backup. Turn off power and the HVAC system to the affected area only if you can do so without touching contaminated water. Skip the mop and bucket, scrub any exposed skin and wait for a trained crew.
Sewage backups are extremely dangerous. Raw sewage contains bacteria, viruses and parasites that can cause serious illness, including hepatitis, E. coli infection and respiratory disease. Do not try cleaning it up unless you have proper protective equipment. For professional cleanup, reach our team at (608) 305-8466 without delay.

Maintenance Tips for Sewage Cleanup

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Every 2 to 3 years, get a sewer camera run through the line to spot developing issues
  • In an emergency a plumber needs the cleanout, so learn where yours is and keep it in good shape
  • Address slow drains promptly, before they become complete blockages
  • Hair and other debris are easy to stop with simple strainers on tub, shower and sink drains
  • Roots from trees close to the sewer line work their way into pipes and block them, so have them cleared
  • A backwater valve at the sewer connection stops the city system from backing up into your home

Signs You Need Sewage Cleanup in Lynn

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Toilets or drains that gurgle whenever you run water somewhere else
  • A cleanout that weeps sewage, or wet foul patches in the lawn along the sewer path
  • Carpet or flooring contaminated when a toilet overflowed with solid waste
  • Multiple drains in your home backing up at the same time
  • Damp patches on a wall or floor that also smell foul
  • A rotten egg or sewer smell from the drains or the lowest floor of the home
  • Dark, dirty water or sewage rising out of a tub, toilet or floor drain

The Benefits of Professional Sewage Cleanup

Sewage cleanup is not something any homeowner should attempt. Handling raw sewage safely takes specialized training, EPA-registered disinfectants that consumers cannot buy, and personal protective equipment, meaning respirators, Tyvek suits and protective boots and gloves. Hepatitis, gastrointestinal disease and infections of the skin or respiratory system are among the serious illnesses these pathogens cause.

Professional sewage cleanup also requires proper disposal of contaminated materials as biohazard waste. Simply placing sewage-contaminated drywall, carpet and insulation in your regular trash is both illegal and hazardous to sanitation workers and the community. A certified restoration company follows proper disposal protocols and works with licensed facilities that accept biohazard waste.
